About

About me.

I'm a Toronto-based AI engineer with a background in machine learning, data analytics, and computer science research.

What I work on

At Environics Analytics, my work has moved from geospatial data and product-quality investigations into AI application engineering. I've worked on model evaluation, tool interfaces, application development, and data processing. Some projects were mine to design and implement; others were team efforts.

I am interested in tests that show where software breaks, especially when language models are involved. I prefer a baseline, a controlled comparison, and a clear description of what a result does not establish.

Education

I earned a BSc in Computer Science with a Mathematics minor and an MSc in Computer Science at Ontario Tech University. My thesis, Discovery of Trend Dependencies Over Time-Series, studied approximate mathematical relationships in time-series data.

My public code includes document question answering, data-pipeline testing, and piano transcription. They're different problems, but each involves making a rough output measurable and usable.
