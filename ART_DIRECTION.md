# Atelier 09 — art direction

## Principle

A personal, self-directed creative-technical practice—not a résumé landing page and not a decorative dashboard. The writing should be restrained and specific; the work should be visually different in each chapter.

## Identity

- **Opening:** editorial `Work & experiments.` and Nicholas Bode's identity in the supporting text, with current employment visible immediately below the hero
- **Dark canvas:** plum ink `#17141e`; **type:** warm parchment `#f5eee2`; **accent:** vermilion `#ff795c`; **material colors:** lilac `#b3a1fb`, warm apricot `#f7b998`, saturated violet in the gallery
- **Light research section:** lilac paper `#ddd2ea` to change the reading pace
- **Materiality:** an independently generated kinetic folded ribbon, dense fine lines, softly graded project scenes, and printed texture—not a generic icon set
- **Interior pages:** conventional accessible long-form typesetting, because the technical evidence is the point

## Layout and tone

The hero has substantial negative space and a large sculpture on desktop; on mobile, typography precedes a full-width tappable scene. The project collection uses three individual environments: purple for RAG, salmon for the incremental data pipeline, blue for piano notation. The work and research articles remain discoverable without animation. Nothing depends on hovering.

## Reference interpretation

Christen Dijoux's laboratory demonstrates original computational artwork and the coordination of large typesetting with detailed micro-motion. Roberto Attanasio's work demonstrates how a personal site can give different interests their own spatial chapters and make navigation feel architectural. This project borrows the principles, **not** any reference artwork, shaders, CSS, fonts, wording or code.

## Deliberate non-goals

No scroll-jacking, perpetual GPU-heavy raymarch, overdone neon gradient buttons, unapproved employer implementation details, claims of external benchmarking without permission, fabricated demos, or abstract marketing copy.
